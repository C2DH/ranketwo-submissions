#### Metadata - a term with roots in computer science

First occurrrence of the word: 1968 in computer science:  
"the ability of programming languages to associate explicitly with a data element a second data element which represents data “about” the first data element. This second data element we might term a “metadata element”. Philip Bagley. Extension of Programming Language Concepts, 1968

Generalization of the term in computer science research from 1970s on. (Gartner 2016: 2-3)

#### Metadata - an instrument in (cultural) information management 
Descriptive instruments (catalogs, index cards…) have been invented wherever/whenever some kind of records have existed in any materiality: 

* Royal Library of Ashurbanipal, Nineveh, Assyrian Empire, 7th c. A.D. (clay trays)
* Library of Alexandria, Callimachus of Cyrene, Pinakes, 3rd c. A.S. (papyrus)
* Europe (Augsburg), 16th c. (context: rise of volume of printed books after the invention of typography)
* First printed catalog of a library: Bodleian, Oxford, 1605
* Libraries (and archives) of nation states, mainly from the 19th c. on
* Paul Otlet, Traité de documentation, 1934
* Development of descriptive standards for the content or the structure of the records since the 1960s: AACR 1967 (RDA 2010), ISBD 1971, MARC 1968...

#### Metadata - definitions 

96 separate ISO standards that provided 46 different definitions. (Furner 2020)

* [Data about other data / information about information](https://www.isko.org/cyclo/metadata#3)
* Data describing other data, for ex. "[Metadata is descriptive information; in other words, it’s information that describes sources such as documents, images and objects.](https://docs.tropy.org/before-you-begin/metadata#what-is-metadata-and-why-is-it-important)"
* Data about other data providing contextual information (on creation, structure, use...) 

**Context, as expressed by metadata, can become more important than content, since it allows for content to be reconstructed.** (Pacheco, A., Da Silva, C.G. & De Freitas, M.C.V. A metadata model for authenticity in digital archival descriptions. Arch Sci 23, 629–673 (2023). https://doi.org/10.1007/s10502-023-09422-w) 

#### Metadata - a human construct 

"There is nothing objective about metadata: it always makes a statement about the world, and this statement is subjective is what it includes, what it omits, where it draws its boundaries and in the terms it uses to describe it." (Gartner 2016: 4)
